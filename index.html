Hindsight Research

What did pundits and analysts believe? How accurate were they?

We read three kinds of forecast:

  • Quantifying vibes. Most commentary does not give numerical probabilities, but still contains information. For these questions, we use LLMs to estimate the authors' implicit forecasts and convert them into probabilities. This allows us to create indices of "conventional wisdom". Whenever possible, we match these indices to data from prediction markets or Metaculus.
  • Explicit forecasts. We evaluate cases where authors provide explicit forecasts. Currently, we have only evaluated predictions from some Apple analysts, who predict when the company will release a given product. These are not especially important questions, but they are easy to grade and useful for tuning our harness.
  • Essay evaluations. This evaluates the forecasting accuracy of longer essays such as "AI 2027" or "Situational Awareness". More calibration work is needed.

Eventually, we hope to integrate all three kinds of forecast into a single dataset. For now, the most promising work has come from our efforts to quantify vibes. Below are nine forecasting questions showing how this approach works across a variety of policy-relevant topics.
