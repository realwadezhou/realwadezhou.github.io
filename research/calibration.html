How well did the pundits (as inferred by LLMs) do as forecasters?

We grade authors on a few metrics:

  • Brier score: Lower is better. 0 is a perfect record; 0.25 is what you would get by always saying 50%.
  • Δ vs consensus: The Brier score of the forecast minus that of a consensus, an average of views up until that point. Negative scores mean the author beat the consensus.
  • Boldness: How far the author’s forecasts sat from 50%. A boldness of 0% means they never leaned either way, while 50% means every call they made was a flat yes or no.
  • Bits vs consensus: How much better (in bits) the author did than the consensus, per forecast. Positive means they beat it; confident calls against the crowd that turn out right earn the most.
