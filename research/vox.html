THE PROJECT

Vox pilot

A lot of calibration work is needed on our harness. This page shows efforts on just one test.

Vox’s "X things we think will happen in 20xx" is chosen as a test case because they include both explicit probability values as well as verbal reasoning. This helps us test our calibration.

The stated probabilities come from Vox. Model and human estimates are inferred. Export: Nov 14, 2026. All analyses below use the full pilot; filters apply only to the item table.

Outcome-directed bias

Mean of (mid − stated probability) × (+1 if happened, −1 otherwise), over resolved items with a midpoint. Shown as mean ± standard error (sample SD / √n), in percentage points.

Positive means the model's estimate leans toward what actually happened, which it could only know from hindsight. Llama 3.1 70B's training data ends in Dec 2023; Claude Sonnet 5.5 has seen all of these outcomes.

This is a leakage diagnostic, not proof of leakage: directional errors alone cannot establish their cause. Unresolved items are excluded.
