AI 2027

Daniel Kokotajlo, Scott Alexander, Thomas Larsen, Eli Lifland & Romeo DeanPublished Apr 3, 2025Read the essay

AI summary: AI 2027 follows a feedback loop: better coding agents speed up AI research, which in turn produces even more capable agents. In the near term, the scenario moves from unreliable assistants to increasingly automated research, playing out alongside a massive buildout of computing power and a US–China security race.

After a suspected alignment failure, the story splits into two paths. Continuing to race leads to a loss of human control. Slowing down gives humanity time to build a safer replacement and negotiate a transition—though it leaves open the question of who ultimately holds political power.

Key assumptions:

  • We assume OpenBrain means any leading US lab, while DeepCent means any leading Chinese lab.
  • We judge the forecasts originally published in April 2025, and not any later revisions.
  • We treat most forecasted numbers as minimums. Reaching or beating the number by the deadline counts as a hit.
