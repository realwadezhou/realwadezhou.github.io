AI 2040: Plan A

Thomas Larsen, Romeo Dean, Brendan Halstead, Eli Lifland, Ryan Greenblatt & Daniel KokotajloPublished Jul 9, 2026Read the essay

AI summary: Plan A proposes an international agreement to slow the transition to superintelligence. Verification, public research, a wider distribution of frontier capability and the ability to disable compute are intended to prevent a secret race or permanent monopoly.

The scenario explores what follows if the agreement works: substantial automation and economic growth during a managed transition, a pause near top-human capability, and a later move to superintelligence. Its predicted benefits depend on difficult technical, political and institutional assumptions.

Key assumptions:

  • Most of this essay describes what happens if the world adopts Plan A, which the authors recommend rather than predict. Those claims are marked conditional and not graded unless a deal like it happens.
  • We judge the forecasts originally published in July 2026, and not any later revisions.
  • We treat most forecasted numbers as minimums. Reaching or beating the number by the deadline counts as a hit.
