AI 2040: Plan A

A proposed path to a slower, internationally governed transition.

Thomas Larsen, Romeo Dean, Brendan Halstead, Eli Lifland, Ryan Greenblatt & Daniel KokotajloPublished Jul 9, 2026Read the essay

Plan A proposes an international agreement to slow the transition to superintelligence. Verification, public research, a wider distribution of frontier capability and the ability to disable compute are intended to prevent a secret race or permanent monopoly.

The scenario explores what follows if the agreement works: substantial automation and economic growth during a managed transition, a pause near top-human capability, and a later move to superintelligence. Its predicted benefits depend on difficult technical, political and institutional assumptions.

Policy recommendation · conditional scenario

The authors explicitly describe policy adoption as a recommendation, not their expected future. Proposed treaty provisions are listed separately; predicted consequences are conditional. The scenario’s 2030 default timeline is not a revision that erases AI 2027, and 2040 is the proposed managed arrival date.

Hardware evidence · Epoch AI

We use Epoch AI as the canonical source for GPU quantities and compute capacity. Its estimates can resolve a claim; an audited inventory is not required. Each comparison states the metric, observation date and interpretation.

Units, coverage and resolution rules

H100-equivalents measure compute capacity, not physical chip counts. Epoch currently normalizes dense 8-bit throughput, with 16-bit fallback for chips without 8-bit support. Training FLOP also depends on utilization and duration. Chip deliveries, deployed stock and compute rented by a lab are separate measures.

Use dated operational capacity, not projected expansion. Distinguish chip power, IT power and total facility power. Epoch’s site list covers part of the world; its sum is not a global total. Missing estimates are not zero. Approximate milestones can be graded once the explicit interpretation is satisfied, even before their window closes.

For Situational Awareness’s buildout table, one operational AI site is our declared proxy for a training cluster. The stricter claim that all capacity is interconnected for a single training job remains unassessed. The GPU, power and cost rows describe the same buildout and are correlated.

How these evaluations work

This initial extraction covers the 2027–2040 narrative, principal economic quantities, four policy principles and selected technical dependencies from the complete 90-page PDF and linked supplements. Alternative failure branches and the speculative post-2040 epilogue are represented as conditional claims, not mutually compatible predictions. Dollar figures in this scenario are real 2025 dollars.

All claim titles are paraphrases. The resolution tests below were written on Sep 5, 2026, after publication; they were not registered by the authors. Approximate dates and numerical tolerances need explicit interpretation. A later author update does not move an original deadline.

Pending means the forecast window or stated condition is still open. Not assessed means essential evidence or interpretation is missing, including passed deadlines where non-occurrence has not been established. A proposal is not a prediction. Branches and related subclaims share a family and must not be counted as independent confirmations.

The cited original passages were reviewed. Linked outcome evidence is described at claim level; a vendor announcement establishes its release or reported result, not independently verified general capability. No pre-publication archive or second human review is recorded. Sources were accessed on Sep 5, 2026.
